# Roche Lobe Overflow

The process of [[mass transfer]] in a binary system between a [[donor]] and [[accretor]] star. This is due to the [[donor]] star overflowing its roche lobe.

Types include
- Conservative Roche lobe overflow
- Case A RLO
- Case B RLO
