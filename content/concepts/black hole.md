# BH

A black hole. A point in space where the force of gravity is so strong that not even light can escape

## Singularity

The mass "in" the BH is in the form of a singularity, i.e. a infinitely dense point. This was predicted by [[GR]], however, it most likely isn't truly infinite, instead GR/gravity needs to be reconciled with quantum mechanics
