# Author's Note

Firstly, thank you so much for taking the time to check this out :), I really appreciate it. If you have any questions at all, please reach out, always love talking about research.

This serves as a simplified, easier and shorter to read version of the full paper ([Kiato et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2610.07152))^[this feels insane to be able to do]. I wanted to make this both as an excuse to do academic writing, but for anyone interested in the research I've done to actually be able to understand and read. Additionally, I decided to format and organize this in the form of an obsidian quartz vault instead of a standard paper format, as I decided that a paper format would make it much more intimidating and that a more scatterbrain/non-linear approach would make the concepts easier to understand.

Also, due to the nature of astrophysics, there will (inevitably and unfortunately) be a lot of jargon. To help combat this, jargon and larger 'precursor info' topics (such as [[Binary Stars|binary stars]]) get their own notes that you can click through to. This is mostly to avoid tangents that distract from the currently discussed topic, but hopefully also to introduce a more broad understanding of the topic.
